If something you lived through still shows up in your sleep, your relationships, or your body's alarm going off when you are safe, you are not broken and you are not stuck for good. At SoundHeart Counseling in the Mat-Su Valley, we offer EMDR and trauma informed therapy that helps your brain finally process what it could not at the time.

Trauma is treatable

Trauma is one of the most treatable conditions in mental health, and yet so many people carry it for years thinking that is just how they are. Whether your trauma came from a single event or from years of difficult experiences, we help Wasilla and Mat-Su clients move from surviving to genuinely healing.

What we help with

  • PTSD and the alarm system that will not switch off.
  • The adult effects of childhood trauma, including anxiety and people pleasing.
  • Recovery after emotional abuse and toxic relationships.
  • Trauma carried by first responders and those whose work follows them home.

What is EMDR?

EMDR is a structured, well researched therapy that helps the brain reprocess stuck memories so they stop feeling like they are happening now. You do not have to retell every detail, and the work is paced to feel manageable. Many people find significant relief, sometimes quickly. Learn more in what is EMDR and does it really work, and about the hope on the other side in post-traumatic growth.

In person in the Valley, or by telehealth

We offer trauma therapy in person for Wasilla and Palmer clients, and by secure telehealth across Alaska. Because healing happens best in safety, we go at your pace, building steadiness first.

What to expect at your first session

Reaching out is the hardest step, so we keep the first one simple. Your first session is an unhurried conversation to understand what brought you in and what you are hoping for. There is no pressure to tell your whole story at once. It is also your chance to see whether we are the right fit, because the relationship between you and your counselor matters more than anything else. Sessions run about fifty minutes, in person or by secure video, and we will explain confidentiality and next steps clearly. You can read more in what to expect in your first therapy session.

Common questions

Do I have to talk about my trauma in detail for EMDR?

No. EMDR does not require retelling the trauma in depth. The focus is on how the memory is stored, and early sessions center on building safety and trust before any processing begins.

How many EMDR sessions will I need?

It depends on your history. A single event trauma can sometimes resolve in a handful of sessions, while complex or repeated trauma takes longer. We tailor the pace to you.

Can EMDR help complex or repeated trauma?

Yes. While it usually takes longer than for a single event, EMDR is effective for complex trauma built up over years. We build safety and stability first, then process at a pace that works for you.
