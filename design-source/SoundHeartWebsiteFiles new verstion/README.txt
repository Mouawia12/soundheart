SoundHeart Counseling, Website Files
====================================

These are the finished design prototypes (plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript).
Please rebuild this design on the chosen platform and port it faithfully.
Do not reverse-engineer the raw HTML; deliver clean, componentized code.

NAVIGATION (identical on every page)
- Header: About, Services, Retreats, Training, Resources, Shop, a Client Login
  link, and a Get Started button.
- Get Started button links to the Get Started page (how it works, fees, FAQ, and
  the contact form). The floating corner buttons are WhatsApp (primary contact for
  international clients) and Book (direct to booking).
- Footer: Contact (to the Get Started page), FAQ, Privacy, Terms, Disclaimer,
  and Client Login.

COMPLIANCE

- The contact form collects name, email, and message only, routed securely.
- HTTPS throughout. Privacy Policy and HIPAA Notice linked in the footer.
