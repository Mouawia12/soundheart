Home / Services / Couples Therapy in Anchorage, Alaska

Anchorage life moves fast, and relationships can quietly slip into conflict or distance while everything else takes priority. At SoundHeart Counseling, we help Anchorage couples slow down, understand what is really happening between them, and rebuild the connection underneath the busyness.

Support for Anchorage couples

We work with Anchorage couples on the things that erode relationships over time, poor communication, recurring fights, fading intimacy, and the loneliness of feeling unseen by your own partner. We also help couples in acute crisis, including those recovering from an affair or trying to decide whether to stay together.

What we help couples with

  • The same recurring argument that never gets resolved.
  • Emotional distance and feeling more like roommates than partners.
  • Rebuilding trust after betrayal.
  • Deciding whether to stay through discernment counseling.

Telehealth that fits Anchorage life

Secure telehealth means Anchorage couples can do meaningful work from home, without a commute or a waiting room, and research shows it is as effective as in person care for most couples. For couples who want to go deeper, our couples retreats in the nearby Mat-Su Valley offer a focused reset away from daily distractions.

The NeuroRelational difference

Our NeuroRelational Belonging approach treats connection as something you practice, not something you either have or do not. We help you build the safety and repair skills that keep a relationship strong, and you can read more about why couples therapy works.

What to expect at your first session

Reaching out is the hardest step, so we keep the first one simple. Your first session is an unhurried conversation to understand what brought you in and what you are hoping for. There is no pressure to tell your whole story at once. It is also your chance to see whether we are the right fit, because the relationship between you and your counselor matters more than anything else. Sessions run about fifty minutes, in person or by secure video, and we will explain confidentiality and next steps clearly. You can read more in what to expect in your first therapy session.

Common questions

Is online couples therapy as good as in person?

For most couples, yes. Research shows telehealth is as effective as in person therapy, and the connection between you and your counselor forms well over a good video connection. It is also far more convenient for busy Anchorage schedules.

We are not in crisis, is it still worth coming?

Absolutely. Couples therapy is not only for relationships on the brink. In fact it works best when couples come earlier, while there is still goodwill to build on, rather than waiting years.

Can a retreat help more than weekly sessions?

For some couples, yes. A retreat concentrates real work into a focused block of time, which can break through patterns that weekly sessions cannot. Many Anchorage couples pair a retreat in the nearby Valley with ongoing telehealth.
