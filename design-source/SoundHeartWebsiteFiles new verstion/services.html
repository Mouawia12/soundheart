Ways we can work together

Licensed therapy here in the Valley, model-based coaching more broadly, professional training, and the thinking that holds it all together.

Everything we offer grows from one idea: connection is not something you find, it is something you practice. Here is how that shows up in the work, and how to find the right fit for you.

CounselingLicensed therapy

Individual, couples, and family therapy for anxiety, depression, trauma, relationships, co-parenting, and more, grounded in the NeuroRelational Belonging model. Counseling is licensed therapy, provided in person here in the Mat-Su Valley and online for clients within Alaska.

Explore counseling →
CoachingFor growth, wherever you are

Coaching is a forward-looking partnership focused on your goals, not a diagnosis or treatment. Because coaching is not licensed therapy, we can offer it more broadly, including to clients outside Alaska and internationally, drawing on the same NeuroRelational Belonging model.

It suits people who want support with relationships, communication, life transitions, and personal growth, and who are not seeking treatment for a mental health condition. If what you need is therapy for a clinical concern, we will say so honestly and help you find the right care.

NeuroRelational Belonging training and certification for clinicians, counselors, coaches, and other helping professionals who want to bring the model into their own work.

ResearchThe thinking behind the model

The NeuroRelational Belonging model draws on research in attachment, trauma, and the nervous system, the idea that safety and belonging are learned, in relationship, over time. It is the throughline beneath everything above.
