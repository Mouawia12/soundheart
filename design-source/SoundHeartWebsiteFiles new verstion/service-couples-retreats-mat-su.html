Home / Services / Couples Retreats in Alaska: What Happens, What It Costs, and Whether It Is Worth It

If weekly counseling feels too slow for what your relationship is facing, a couples retreat may be exactly what you need. A retreat gives you focused, uninterrupted time to get past the surface fight and reach the thing underneath, guided the whole way. Here are honest answers to the questions couples ask most before booking one.

What happens during a couples retreat?

Instead of fifty minutes once a week, a retreat gives you concentrated time over several days to work through what is really going on, with your counselor guiding you the entire way. There is room to slow down, get past the argument you always have, and practice a new way of being together before the time is up. Couples leave having actually done the work, not just talked about it.

Is a couples retreat worth it?

For the right situation, yes. Weekly sessions can stall when each one gets reset by the next crisis before progress takes hold. A retreat removes those interruptions and lets change build on itself. It is especially worth it when a relationship is in real crisis, when trust has been broken, or when distance and busy schedules make consistent weekly sessions hard, which is common across Alaska.

Can a retreat save a marriage?

A retreat cannot do the work for you, but it can be the thing that finally gets a stuck couple unstuck. Many couples find that concentrated time, in a safe and guided space, breaks a pattern that years of weekly attempts could not. It is often the turning point, followed by lighter ongoing work.

The 3-day and the 5-day retreat

Our 3-day retreat is the standard for most couples, enough time to reconnect, break a recurring pattern, or strengthen a solid relationship. Our 5-day retreat is designed for couples recovering from a crisis or a breach of trust, such as an affair, where rebuilding trust needs more time. You can read a full comparison in 3-day vs 5-day couples retreat, and how retreats compare to weekly work in intensive or weekly.

What does a couples retreat cost?

Retreat pricing depends on the length and format that fit your situation, and it is private pay. We are always happy to talk openly about cost and help you choose the option that genuinely fits, never the longer one just because it is longer. Reach out and we will walk you through it.

What to expect at your first session

Reaching out is the hardest step, so we keep the first one simple. Your first session is an unhurried conversation to understand what brought you in and what you are hoping for. There is no pressure to tell your whole story at once. It is also your chance to see whether we are the right fit, because the relationship between you and your counselor matters more than anything else. Sessions run about fifty minutes, in person or by secure video, and we will explain confidentiality and next steps clearly. You can read more in what to expect in your first therapy session.

Common questions

What happens during a couples retreat?

You spend focused, guided time over several days working through what is really going on, with room to get past the surface argument and practice new patterns. Couples leave having done the work, not just discussed it.

Is a marriage retreat worth the cost?

For couples in crisis, or those stuck in patterns weekly sessions cannot touch, it often is. A retreat removes the weekly reset and lets change build on itself, which frequently becomes the turning point.

Should we choose the 3-day or 5-day retreat?

The 3-day retreat suits most couples reconnecting or strengthening a solid relationship. The 5-day is designed for recovery from a crisis or a breach of trust like an affair, where rebuilding trust needs more time. We will help you decide.
