Home / Services / Counseling in Eagle River: Individuals, Couples, and Military Families Near JBER

Eagle River sits right between Joint Base Elmendorf Richardson and the mountains, home to many service members, veterans, and their families, alongside everyone else who loves the area. We support this community through the unique strains of military life and the ordinary hard seasons every person and relationship faces.

Support for military couples and families

Military life asks a lot of relationships, long separations, the vigilance of deployment, frequent moves that uproot careers and kids, and the real work of becoming partners again after time apart. None of that means anything is wrong with your marriage. It means you are carrying an unusual load, and we understand it. Read more in reconnecting after deployment.

What we help with

  • Reconnecting after deployment and the strain of emotional withdrawal.
  • Deployment related stress and PTSD, including EMDR.
  • Trauma carried by first responders and service members.
  • Individual support for anxiety, depression, and men's mental health.

Care that fits military life

Because moves and schedules are a fact of military life, we offer secure telehealth across Alaska so your care can continue through change. TRICARE and coverage questions are welcome, and we will help you understand your options.

For the whole Eagle River community

You do not have to be connected to the base to work with us. We support individuals, couples, and families throughout Eagle River and Anchorage, in person nearby and by telehealth.

What to expect at your first session

Reaching out is the hardest step, so we keep the first one simple. Your first session is an unhurried conversation to understand what brought you in and what you are hoping for. There is no pressure to tell your whole story at once. It is also your chance to see whether we are the right fit, because the relationship between you and your counselor matters more than anything else. Sessions run about fifty minutes, in person or by secure video, and we will explain confidentiality and next steps clearly. You can read more in what to expect in your first therapy session.

Common questions

Do you work with military families near JBER?

Yes. We support service members, veterans, and their families in Eagle River, near JBER, and across Anchorage, with deployment, reintegration, trauma, and the everyday stresses of military life, in person nearby and by telehealth.

Why is reconnecting after deployment so hard?

Both partners change during a long separation. Coming back together means renegotiating roles and identities, not simply resuming where you left off, which can create distance or conflict for a while. Support helps you find your way back.

Does TRICARE cover counseling?

Coverage varies by plan and service. Reach out with your details and we will help you understand your options, including which services are billable and which are private pay.
