Trauma has a way of staying present long after the danger has passed, in your sleep, your reactions, your relationships. If that is your experience, here is the hopeful truth. Trauma is highly treatable, and with the right support your nervous system can finally learn that it is safe. We offer trauma therapy and EMDR right here in Palmer and across the Mat-Su Valley.

You are not stuck for good

Many people carry trauma for years, quietly assuming this is just how they are. It is not. Whether your trauma came from one overwhelming event or from years of hard experiences, healing is possible, and it does not require you to relive every detail to get there.

What we help Palmer clients with

  • PTSD and a body that stays on high alert.
  • The adult effects of childhood trauma, including anxiety, perfectionism, and people pleasing.
  • Healing after emotional abuse and toxic relationships.
  • The weight of family secrets and generational patterns.

How EMDR helps

EMDR is one of the most researched trauma treatments, and it helps the brain reprocess stuck memories so they stop feeling like they are happening now. The work is paced and manageable, and many people find real relief. Read more in what is EMDR, and about what becomes possible after healing in post-traumatic growth.

Palmer based, Valley wide

We are based in the Mat-Su Valley, so Palmer clients can work with us in person, and we offer secure telehealth across Alaska for anyone who prefers or needs it. We go at your pace, building safety first, because that is how lasting healing happens.

What to expect at your first session

Reaching out is the hardest step, so we keep the first one simple. Your first session is an unhurried conversation to understand what brought you in and what you are hoping for. There is no pressure to tell your whole story at once. It is also your chance to see whether we are the right fit, because the relationship between you and your counselor matters more than anything else. Sessions run about fifty minutes, in person or by secure video, and we will explain confidentiality and next steps clearly. You can read more in what to expect in your first therapy session.

Common questions

Is EMDR available in Palmer?

Yes. We offer EMDR and trauma informed therapy for Palmer and the wider Mat-Su Valley, in person and by secure telehealth across Alaska.

Will trauma therapy make me relive everything?

No. Good trauma therapy, including EMDR, is paced to feel manageable and does not require reliving every detail. We build safety and grounding first, then process at a pace that works for you.

Can childhood trauma still affect me as an adult?

Very much. Early trauma often shows up later as anxiety, perfectionism, people pleasing, or difficulty trusting. The good news is that it responds well to treatment at any age.
